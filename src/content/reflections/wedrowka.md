## What I did
One week, instead of my usual swim, I went hiking. It kept me active and outdoors
for a few hours and gave my shoulders a rest from the pool.

## Reflection
Swapping one session for a hike reminded me that staying active does not have to
mean the same thing every time, and that mixing it up can actually keep me going
for longer. I came back to swimming the next week feeling fresher.
