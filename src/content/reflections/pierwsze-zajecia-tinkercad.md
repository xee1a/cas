## What I did
On Wednesday I ran the first class. I introduced the kids to 3D design in
Tinkercad, showing them how to move around the workplane, add and resize shapes,
and join them into a simple first model. Everyone logged into their own account
and had a go at building something themselves.

## Reflection
Explaining something I find easy to a room of beginners turned out to be a real
skill in itself, and I had to slow right down and keep checking that everyone was
keeping up. Watching the kids get excited when their first shape appeared made
all the planning feel worth it.
