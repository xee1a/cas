## Before I started
I wanted to share something I enjoy with younger kids, so I set up a series of 3D
design and printing classes for children. Before anything else I needed a clear
idea of what we would actually do in each session.

## Planning
In week three of CAS I prepared the lesson plan. I laid out the topics, the
order, and what the kids will learn in each session, from the first idea, through
modelling, to the finished print. The plan is attached below as evidence.

## Reflection
Putting the plan together was harder than I expected because I had to imagine how
a complete beginner would follow each step. It made me think carefully about
pacing things so the kids stay interested instead of getting lost.
