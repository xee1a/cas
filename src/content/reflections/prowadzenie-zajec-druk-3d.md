## What I have been doing
Since the first session I have been running the 3D design and printing classes
every Wednesday. The only week I missed was 11 November, because it was
Independence Day, a national holiday in Poland. That week I still kept working on
the classes, planning what we would do in future sessions and fixing some of the
kids' projects so they would be ready to print.

## Evidence
The photos from the classes will be available once this round with the kids
finishes, because the owner of the Centrum Aktywności Lokalnej, Michał, will post
them then. If evidence is needed sooner, he can be contacted directly (Michał,
Centrum Aktywności Lokalnej Widawa, Wrocław).

## Reflection
Keeping the classes going every week has shown me how much steady commitment a
real project needs, especially when a holiday or a tricky model could easily
break the rhythm. Fixing the kids' projects between sessions also made me realise
how much of the work happens quietly outside the lessons themselves.
