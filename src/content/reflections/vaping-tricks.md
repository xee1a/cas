## Before I started
My goal was to move beyond simply exhaling and into a genuinely original,
interpretive performance. I planned a progression: O-rings first, then the
ghost, then the French inhale, and finally the legendary tornado.

## What I did
I practised daily in carefully chosen windless conditions. The O-rings took
two weeks; the ghost took three. The tornado remains a work in progress and
mostly just looks like a cloud.

## Reflection
This experience pushed me well outside my comfort zone and taught me new skills
(LO2). There were many failed rings and a lot of coughing, but I kept going
(LO4). Next step: synchronised routines, and possibly a flavour-based artistic
statement.
